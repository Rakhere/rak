Agri-Tech Systems Architecture · Python & Django

AgriHelp — Farm Intelligence Platform

Problem Statement

Smallholder farmers frequently lack accessible financial calculation tools to evaluate their gross margins before and after harvest. Unpredictable input costs (seeds, synthetic fertilizers, machinery rentals, and labor) often lead to debt cycles and mispriced market sales.

The Python Engine

Engineered core computational functions in Python that process variable and fixed expenditures against projected or realized agricultural yields and market rates, outputting itemized profit, loss, and margin percentages.

Technical Stack

Python Django 5.2 Google Gemini AI MySQL PWA 10+ Regional Languages GitHub

Integrated Dashboard Features

  • Multilingual Support: Localized across over 10 regional Indian languages (Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, etc.) ensuring inclusive rural access.
  • Today's Crop Advisory: Automated agronomy notifications, rust symptom surveillance, and irrigation timing.
  • Ask AgriBot (एग्रीबॉट): Conversational assistant for real-time pest control and soil diagnosis.
  • Mandi Rates (मंडी भाव): Live local market price benchmarks for Wheat, Rice, and Sugarcane.
  • Weather & Disease Early Warning: Predictive micro-climate risk indexes (Low/Moderate/High risk alert tiers).
